# Teddy v0.6 — grounded articulated anatomy

The reference screenshot was treated as evidence, not as a target to cosmetically imitate. Four visible failure classes drive this slice: disconnected primitive intersections, flattened front-on depth, accidental translucency/order artifacts, and a seated anatomy that cannot support credible arm/leg locomotion.

## Intent decomposition

1. **Bear physiognomy:** preserve the compact eye/nose triangle and broad muzzle while reducing mask-like head mass and oversized feet.
2. **Continuous plush anatomy:** hide joints inside overlapping soft volumes; never expose spherical hinge gaps.
3. **Articulation:** explicit pelvis/spine/neck/head/jaw, shoulder/elbow/wrist and hip/knee/ankle chains.
4. **Perspective:** use an elevated look-at camera and narrower perspective instead of a pure translated camera.
5. **Opacity:** Teddy is rendered in an opaque depth-writing pass. Blending is enabled only for the floor contact shadow.
6. **Grounding:** an explicit floor plane and bounded gait phase provide a stable stance and future locomotion seam.
7. **Voice coupling:** remote-audio expression continues to drive jaw and restrained upper-body gesture; locomotion and speech share one bounded transform hierarchy.
8. **Degradation:** shader/program failure falls back to Canvas2D instead of killing the renderer.

## Falsification and saturation

`tools/search-anatomy-v6.mjs --full` evaluates exactly **10,000,000 deterministic anatomy identities** against independent hard constraints for head/body, muzzle/head, eye spread, ear/skull overlap, shoulder taper, arm/leg chains and foot proportions. A separate kinematic campaign attacks articulated poses. After the main campaign, the gate executes **M+100** targeted falsifications; CI requires no previously unseen failure class in that window.

The first draft of the constraints rejected every candidate because the ear-overlap metric was internally inconsistent. That was treated as a failed hypothesis and repaired before convergence rather than weakening the final gate.

This remains a synthetic geometry/kinematics search; it does not pretend that ten million browser screenshots were produced. Browser-visible behavior is covered separately by renderer contract tests.
