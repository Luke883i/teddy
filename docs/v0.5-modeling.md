# Teddy v0.5 — morphology and visual reconstruction

The screenshot that triggered this slice was treated as a **5% confidence baseline**. The goal is not “more primitives”; it is an immediately readable teddy bear at rest, in silhouette, at three-quarter view and while speaking.

## Root causes found

1. `M4.translate()` had a malformed Z basis. Translation therefore corrupted camera/model depth and made forward facial anatomy unreliable. v0.5 repairs the homogeneous transform and tests X/Y/Z point transforms directly.
2. The first external validation harness used VTK's default sphere radius `0.5` while Teddy's WebGL primitive has unit radius `1.0`. Those early references falsely exaggerated disconnected anatomy. Final evidence is regenerated with **sphere radius 1.0**, matching renderer scale.

## Morphology saturation

`tools/search-morphology-v5.mjs --full` enumerates exactly one million deterministic identities. The search varies head/body/muzzle ratios, eye and ear placement, arm/hip locations and foot projection. Hard gates discard candidates with non-bear head ratios, weak/overexposed ears, implausible muzzle proportions, poor eye spread, detached arm clearance or insufficient foot projection. Remaining candidates are scored against the converged archetypal target.

Result:

- evaluated: **1,000,000**
- accepted: **325,450**
- rejected: **674,550**
- best identity: **792652**
- digest: `fad399379f2c7cfdc6599b72f5473d5aa64cd8b835b6100601299559e4423049`

This is a deterministic synthetic morphology search, not a claim of one million physical browser renders.

## Graphical verification

The final model was rasterized from the same semantic parts and transform hierarchy at the renderer's **1:1 unit-sphere scale** in an offscreen triangle rasterizer. These references validate geometry, overlap, silhouette and speaking-mouth projection; they are not substitutes for the exact browser GLSL material.
